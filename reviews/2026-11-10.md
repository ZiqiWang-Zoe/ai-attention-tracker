Its 216 uploader-days span all 30 days, with no day contributing more than 13. Aggregate data cannot establish concentration in one recurring uploader.

Other listed risers are spread across multiple days. Reconstructing yesterday’s windows from the current CSVs shows plausible changes, though yesterday’s saved snapshot was not supplied.

The “rising on all three sources” GPU claim needs qualification because the paper matches are noisy.

## Suggested fixes
- Add a paper-specific GPU matching rule requiring substantive hardware, kernel, or GPU performance content instead of accepting every abstract mention of “GPU.”
- Deduplicate HN stories by canonical URL and flag near-identical headlines before counting them independently.
- Check the October 29 paper collection for completeness.
- Generate review counts from the same data and date boundaries as publication. HN seven-day evidence counts disagree with the CSVs: funding 52 versus 51, GPUs 130 versus 129, and inference 19 versus 17.
- Label Claude paper counts as mentions and model shares as uploader-days wherever rankings appear.
