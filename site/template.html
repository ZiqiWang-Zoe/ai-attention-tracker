Method and limits

  • Every Hacker News story, every paper on Hugging Face's curated daily papers page (a selection, not all AI research), and every public model created on the Hugging Face Hub, each day since January 1, 2025.
  • Each item is matched to 59 topics using keyword rules (titles for HN, title and abstract for papers, model name and tags for models). One item can count toward several topics.
  • On Hacker News, generic topics such as "medical" only count when the title also mentions AI.
  • Models are counted by uploader: each account counts once per day, however many models it posts.
  • Rising = share in the last 30 days divided by share in the 90 days before. A topic needs at least 20 items (50 uploaders for models) in the last 30 days to be ranked.
  • Keyword matching misses items that never use the keywords and catches some that use them in another sense.
  • Hacker News titles are short, so every topic is undercounted there. Trends are more reliable than levels.
  • The most recent days on Hugging Face models undercount until the Hub finishes tagging new uploads.
  • All of this is correlation. A rising share says more people wrote or built around a topic, not why.
